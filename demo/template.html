Gnocchi AI · demo

What did each AI investment move?

One year of real, anonymized closed-won deals, with synthetic CRM history and AI logs added on top, run through Gnocchi step by step: from finding the AI a company runs to a renew, consolidate or replace decision for each investment.

Real: 641 + 695 closed-won deals, 2023–2024 Synthetic: CRM history, AI logs, planted effects Team level only
Step 1 · Discover

Find the AI the company already runs

Read-only connections to the CRM, the OKR source and the AI sources. Agent identities in the CRM's change history show acting AI; admin and usage data show the tools people use directly. The result is an inventory of AI investments by team.

Sample logs: one deal's change history and one session per tool

  






  
Step 2 · Map
Link each key result to the records that hold its progress

  
The semantic layer reads each team OKR and proposes where its progress is recorded, which fields carry it and at what grain. A person confirms. A key result with no record is flagged as a blind spot. Shown: EMEA's 2024 OKR.

  

  






  
Step 3 · Learn
Find what actually moves the target

  
From two years of change history, Gnocchi learns which actions, roles and materials shorten the new-business sales cycle (key result 3), controlling for deal size, team and month. Bars show days added or saved, with 90% ranges.

  

  






  
Step 4 · Trace
Give every AI investment an evidence level

  
Each investment is linked to records (an agent identity's writes, or AI-prepared materials attached to a deal) and to OKRs (session purpose), then triangulated with the OKR map. The level says how strong the link is; impact comes in step 6.

  

  






  
Step 5 · Momentum
See whether each tool is used, and on what

  
An assignment date shows access, not use. Usage, the records each tool touches and the target's driver go on one timeline. The first deliverable, in weeks.

Step 7 · Find
Point to the AI the company needs next

  
The same model, run in reverse: a driver with a high weight, a weak baseline and no AI on it yet is an AI opportunity. Adding a solutions engineer before the proposal saves about  days, and two teams rarely do it.

Step 8 · Report
A decision for every AI investment

  
Per investment: cost, evidence level, impact on the target with its method and confidence, and the recommendation.

Check
Did the analysis find what was planted?

  
The synthetic data carries known effects. Recovering them shows the method works; it says nothing about whether these tools work in a real company.

  






  
About the data. Deals are real and anonymized (owner, account and partner names replaced). CRM history, OKRs, AI logs and usage are synthetic, generated with a fixed seed. Only closed-won deals exist, so win rate isn’t studied. NA West and APAC are small teams, which widens their ranges.

  
Everything is reported at team level. No person is a unit of analysis.
