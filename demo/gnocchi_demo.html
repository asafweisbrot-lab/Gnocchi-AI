AI impact report · Sales · Jan–Dec 2024

What did each AI investment move?

Gnocchi read the CRM, the team OKRs and every AI source the sales org runs, then worked step by step from what the AI is used for to what it changed. It ends with a decision per investment and the open-weights potential.

What this run processed

Read-only connections. Deals are real and anonymized; history, OKRs and AI logs are synthetic, with known effects planted so the result can be checked (appendix).

A
Appendix: did the analysis find what was planted?
The synthetic data carries known effects. Recovering them shows the method works; it says nothing about whether these tools work in a real company.

  





  
About the data. Deals are real and anonymized (owner, account and partner names replaced). CRM history, OKRs, AI logs and usage are synthetic, generated with a fixed seed. Only closed-won deals exist, so win rate isn’t studied. NA West and APAC are small teams, which widens their ranges.

  
Everything is reported at team level. No person is a unit of analysis.
